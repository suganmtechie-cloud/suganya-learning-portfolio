# First 30 days

## Week 1 — Discover
Meet business stakeholders, trainers and SMEs. Review role profiles, curricula, learner data, current assets, delivery constraints and business priorities.

## Week 2 — Diagnose
Map critical tasks to competencies. Identify learner personas, baseline proficiency, prerequisite gaps and priority cohorts.

## Week 3 — Design
Build a pilot learning path with outcomes, Markdown content structure, facilitator guide, hands-on lab, assessment rubric and feedback loop.

## Week 4 — Pilot and report
Facilitate a small cohort, analyse evidence, capture learner/trainer feedback, revise assets and recommend scale-up.

## Measures to agree
Leading: participation, practice completion, objective-level proficiency.  
Lagging: workplace application, quality, cycle time, rework or productivity—only where data and attribution are credible.
