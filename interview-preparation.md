# Interview preparation

## Positioning
Technical L&D Professional | Full-Stack Technology Educator | Learning Experience Designer.

## Core message
My documented experience combines programming education, product training, academic teaching and industry exposure. I would be transparent about the specific full-stack frameworks and tools I have used hands-on, and would not overstate experience in any stack component.

## How I design training
I begin with the business task and learner baseline, define observable outcomes, design examples and hands-on practice, assess performance with a transparent rubric, and use feedback and workplace evidence to improve the programme.

## Markdown-first learning architecture
A module can include a README for outcomes and navigation, concept notes, lab instructions, assessment questions, rubric and reflection. Git provides change history and collaboration. Pair Markdown with rendered pages, diagrams, notebooks or LMS content where richer media or accessibility features are needed.

## STAR stories to prepare
1. HCL Commerce training: audience, learning need, your role, delivery approach, learner evidence and improvement.
2. RPA training for 100+ students: planning, facilitation, learner variation, challenges and verified outcome.
3. Workshops / FDPs / hackathons: stakeholder coordination, timelines and last-minute changes.
4. Project guidance: mentoring, cross-functional collaboration and problem solving.
5. A session that did not go as planned: gap, corrective action and lesson.

Use only metrics you can verify. Do not invent satisfaction scores, pass rates, ROI or years of full-stack development.

## Questions for the panel
- Which capability gaps are most urgent for the first six months?
- How is success measured: proficiency, certification, project readiness or workplace performance?
- Which learner populations and technology stack will this role primarily support?
- How are behavioural capabilities integrated into technical learning?
- What content platforms, LMS and assessment tools are currently used?
