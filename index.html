How I approach learning

Thoughtful design. Active practice.

I keep the learner and the intended workplace outcome at the centre of each programme.

01 / DISCOVER

Understand

Clarify the audience, role expectations, prior knowledge and capability need.

02 / DESIGN

Structure

Set measurable outcomes and sequence concepts, examples, practice and assessment.

03 / DELIVER

Facilitate

Explain, demonstrate, guide hands on work and encourage peer learning.

04 / IMPROVE

Evaluate

Review understanding, learner feedback and opportunities to apply skills at work.
