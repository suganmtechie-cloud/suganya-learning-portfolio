# Assessment and rubric

## Assessment blueprint
- **Before learning:** diagnostic quiz and confidence pulse.
- **During learning:** retrieval checks, code-alongs, pair tasks and instructor observation.
- **After learning:** practical task / capstone aligned to stated outcomes.
- **Transfer:** workplace application check-in after 2–4 weeks, where feasible.

## Sample 4-level rubric
| Criterion | 4 — Strong | 3 — Meets | 2 — Developing | 1 — Needs support |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Correctness | Complete and correct | Minor issue | Several issues | Does not work |
| Reasoning | Explains trade-offs | Explains main choices | Partial explanation | Cannot explain |
| Quality | Clear, maintainable | Mostly clear | Inconsistent | Difficult to follow |
| Collaboration | Proactive, constructive | Participates | Uneven | Does not engage |

Agree criteria and pass thresholds with stakeholders before delivery; do not invent outcome claims after the fact.
